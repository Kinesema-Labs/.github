# Kinesema Labs

**Inspectable tools for language, knowledge, and meaning.**

Kinesema Labs is an early-stage, Deaf-led independent research studio. We explore language structure and the foundations of signing, with accessibility and human review built into the work.

## How we work

We make assumptions, evidence, and limitations visible. The public hand study explores form; language research and signing motion remain in development. Technical checks do not establish linguistic validity: handshapes and signs require Deaf and SgSL linguistic review.

For community review, accessibility feedback, or research collaboration, [contact Kinesema Labs](https://kinesema.com/contact/). Written communication is welcome.
